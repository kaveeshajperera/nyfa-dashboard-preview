The initiative

About the Programme

The National Youth First Aid Training Programme, implemented by the Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka, with support from UNICEF, aims to strengthen community preparedness and emergency response by equipping youth representatives from Grama Niladhari divisions across the country with essential first-aid knowledge and practical skills.

Training is delivered through Community-Based Organisations (CBOs), following the approved national first-aid curriculum. Quality and consistency are supported through hands-on skills training, pre- and post-training assessments, and field supervision coordinated by the Ministry of Health.
